# System Analysis (SA)

## Outcomes
One operational customer record; online application and due diligence; research review/signature/publication; actionable CRM and commission; omnichannel cases; finance and compliance controls.

## Customer and contract lifecycle
LegendFlow creates/identifies applicant. Applicant reviews and signs application/contract package first. Store exact document version/hash, signer assurance, timestamp and consent evidence. Applicant then completes online KYC/customer data, suitability questions and applicable AML/CFT declarations/documents. Automated completeness and consistency checks run. Authorized screening and human review handle exceptions, high-risk cases, possible matches, suitability decisions and approvals. Company acceptance is recorded separately. Contract effective date is determined by approved terms. Payment and entitlement are separate states. Rejection records reason and notice; collected funds enter controlled refund process.

Do not collapse application, customer signature, company acceptance, KYC, suitability, AML, contract, payment and entitlement states.

## Research lifecycle
Sources: Pantheon Persona-autonomous candidate or analyst-initiated research. Candidate includes ticket/plan/artifact/evidence references, source timestamps, version and hash. Candidate is never customer-visible. LongGood creates review case. Analyst can converse with Agora/Servant for revision, evidence, counter-analysis or customer-message draft. Each revision is immutable and linked to prior artifact. Required professional/compliance reviews complete. Analyst signs exact report bytes/hash. Signature verification emits publication event automatically. Audience is resolved from active contract entitlements at publication time. LegendFlow feed and approved messaging channels are updated; delivery statuses recorded per recipient/channel. Corrections/withdrawals create new versions/events, never rewrite history.

## CRM
Track funnel from lead through application, company acceptance, payment, entitlement, renewal, upgrade and churn. Keep original source, maintenance owner, opportunity owner and transaction conversion owner separate. Worklists include incomplete applications, pending review, unpaid, expiring, service issues, renewal, upgrade, churn and Company Pool. Sales commission requires evidenced intervention and successful conversion. Seven days without qualifying maintenance activity returns eligible relationship to Company Pool; employee departure returns immediately. Do not confuse case owner with sales owner.

## Customer service
Web/member, official messaging, email, phone and onsite intake share case identity. Record channel, raw content reference, category, priority, SLA, owner, linked contract/report/payment and resolution. AI can classify, summarize, draft and route; investment judgement, complaint, refund and restricted AML matters use authorized human workflows.

## Finance
Contract/service schedule -> billing -> payment -> settlement/bank reconciliation -> prepayment -> revenue recognition per approved policy -> refund/credit adjustment -> GL export. Cash receipt, settlement and earned revenue are distinct. Commission consumes finance-confirmed eligible untaxed service revenue.

## Internal control
RBAC+ABAC, maker/checker, restricted AML access, append-only audit, vendor least privilege, retention/legal hold. Internal audit is independent and read-only for evidence.

## Non-functional
Targets for SLA, throughput, RPO/RTO, retention and availability are TBD by owners. Require encryption, MFA, secret management, idempotency, durable queues, observability, backup and recovery tests.
